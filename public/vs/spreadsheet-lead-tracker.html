Peak vs a spreadsheet lead tracker

When the next call gets lost between rows.

Last updated November 21, 2026

A spreadsheet can be enough when one person handles a few inquiries and keeps every next step current. You can build your own score formula, assignment column, and follow-up filter without adopting another tool.

Peak brings capture, scoring, lead ownership, and a morning call list into one workflow. Connect a website form or eligible Google lead form, define the scoring points, and keep the assignee and follow-up attached to the lead.

Keep the spreadsheet if…

Your volume is manageable, your formulas are maintained, and the team reliably updates its shared list. There is no reason to switch just to replace a table.

Consider Peak if…

You spend time assembling the call list, new inquiries need manual copying, or ownership and next steps get lost between teammates.
