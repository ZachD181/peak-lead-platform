SECURITY & DATA

Know how your information is handled.

A practical overview of Peak’s current safeguards and the services involved in operating your account.

Last reviewed November 20, 2026

Controls built into Peak

Account access

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sign-in uses a secure, HttpOnly session cookie on the live site. Password-reset links expire, are single-use, and revoke existing sessions after a successful reset.

Business account separation

Authenticated requests are scoped to the signed-in business account. Role checks restrict owner-only actions such as team administration and integration setup.

Integration credentials

Stored lead-connection credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Webhook keys are checked before Google or custom webhook submissions are accepted. Keep your private keys confidential.

Payment handling

Stripe hosts checkout and subscription management. Peak stores billing identifiers and subscription status, rather than full payment-card numbers or card security codes.

Your team’s responsibilities

  • Use a strong, unique password and invite only people who need access.
  • Keep webhook URLs and private verification keys out of public posts and screenshots. Replace an exposed key and update the connected platform.
  • Collect and use lead information according to your business’s privacy notice and permissions.
  • Use fictional details when testing forms or recording product demos.

Privacy requests and security concerns

Email support@peakleadplatform.com with the account or business involved and a description of the issue. For deletion requests, we may need to verify your identity or authority. Canceling a subscription does not automatically delete lead records; some information may need to be retained for billing, security, or legal reasons.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, access tokens, or unnecessary customer records by email.

Scope of this overview

This page describes current application controls, not an independent security audit or certification. Peak does not claim SOC 2 certification or guarantee that these controls alone satisfy every business’s compliance requirements. No service can guarantee complete security.
