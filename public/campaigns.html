Connect a Google Ads lead form

This is a manual lead-form connection. You do not need to share your Google password with Peak.

  1. In your Google Ads account, open the lead form asset you want to connect. Your account must be eligible to use lead forms.
  2. Open the form’s lead delivery or webhook settings. Paste Peak’s Webhook URL into the Webhook URL field.
  3. Paste Peak’s Private verification key into the Google key field. Keep it private.
  4. Have the form collect a name and an email address or phone number. Use your own business’s privacy-policy URL.
  5. Use Google’s Send test data button. Check for success in Google, then open Peak’s Dashboard and confirm the test lead’s details and campaign.

Each connection sends leads to this Peak campaign. Use its own URL and key for every form intended for this campaign. This does not import past leads or connect website contact forms.
