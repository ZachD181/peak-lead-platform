HOW SCORING WORKS

See why a lead moves up your list.

Peak adds points for how soon someone needs help, how ready they are to buy, and whether they’ve provided a phone number and inquiry notes. Every lead starts with 25 points; the total is capped at 100, and missing answers earn no extra points. Your account owner can adjust the points in Settings to reflect what matters to your business. This is a rules-based priority score, not a prediction of a sale; budget confirmation, response speed, source, and pipeline stage do not currently change it.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Know what you’re signing up for.

Is the demo data from real customers?

No. The names, lead records, and performance figures in the homepage demo are fictional examples, labeled SAMPLE DATA. They illustrate the workflow and are not customer results or testimonials. Your own account displays the inquiries you add or receive through connected sources.

What does the trial cost, and what happens afterward?

The trial is free for 14 days after activation in Stripe checkout. Loading current subscription price… A payment method is required to activate the trial, and your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel beforehand through Billing → Manage Subscription. Stripe checkout confirms your exact total and renewal date. Creating an account alone does not activate the trial or charge your card. The introductory offer is for new businesses only, limited to the first 10 trial activations; your place is confirmed when Stripe checkout completes. Review pricing and trial terms →

Can I tune the lead score for my business?

Yes. Your account owner can open Settings → Your scoring preferences and change the points for urgency, buying readiness, a provided phone number, and inquiry notes. Peak uses the saved preferences when scoring leads; it does not learn weights automatically from sales results.

How do I connect Google Ads lead forms?

In Peak, create a Google Ads campaign and open its connection setup to get your private webhook URL and key. In your Google lead form’s Lead delivery option, paste those values and use Send test data, then confirm receipt in Peak. This is a manual lead-form connection; your Google account must be eligible for lead forms. Connecting the form does not launch ads, change your budget, or import past leads.

Does a score of 82 mean an 82% chance of a sale?

No. It means the lead earned 82 points under your scoring rules. Use it to prioritize review and follow-up alongside the person’s actual inquiry. It does not verify budget, guarantee intent, or promise a sale.
