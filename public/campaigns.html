Connect campaign

Send new lead-form submissions into Peak automatically. Creating a connection does not launch ads or change your ad budget.

Use a Page access token authorized for Peak’s Meta app. Peak verifies the Page and form, then subscribes to new lead notifications. The token is encrypted and never shown again.

Finish in Google Ads

  1. Open the campaign’s lead form asset in Google Ads.
  2. In lead delivery, paste the Webhook URL and use the Private verification key as the Google key.
  3. Make the form collect an email address or phone number, plus the lead’s name.
  4. Click Google’s Send test data button, then refresh this connection’s status.

Google test leads are labeled in their activity history. The same delivery received twice creates only one lead.

Google’s lead-form test guide

Finish in Meta

  1. Peak’s app webhook is configured centrally. The saved connection subscribes your Page to leadgen notifications.
  2. Use Meta’s Lead Ads Testing Tool to create a test lead for this Page and form.
  3. Refresh status here, then open Dashboard to see the captured lead.

Receiving a verification challenge is only the first step. A lead must arrive before this connection is proven end to end. Meta may require app review for customers outside your app’s roles.
