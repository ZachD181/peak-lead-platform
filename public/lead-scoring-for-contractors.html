Lead scoring for remodeling contractors

Know who to call first after an inquiry comes in.

Last updated November 21, 2026

For a remodeling or trade business, a new lead might be a homeowner ready for an estimate, someone comparing contractors, or someone researching a future project. Peak helps you put those inquiries in order before the first call.

Start with the answers you actually have.

Peak uses timing, buying readiness, and whether a phone number and inquiry notes are provided. A 25-point base plus your configured points produces a score capped at 100. Missing answers earn no extra points. It is a priority score, not a probability of a sale.

Make the handoff visible.

Assign the lead, record the next step, and schedule a follow-up. Your morning brief brings urgent and overdue opportunities together. Review the homeowner’s request alongside the score before calling.

Connect the source before relying on it.

Website forms, custom webhooks, and eligible Google Ads lead forms are available. Google uses a manual webhook URL and key setup. Facebook/Instagram connections are awaiting approval. Peak does not supply prospect lists or automatically call leads.
