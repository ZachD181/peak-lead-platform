BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few straightforward answers.

When does my trial begin?

Create your account, sign in, and complete Stripe checkout to activate your 14-day trial. Account creation alone does not activate dashboard access or charge your card.

What happens after the trial?

Your subscription renews automatically at the price shown above unless you cancel before the trial ends. Review the billing date and total in Stripe checkout before confirming.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing → Manage Subscription in Peak and complete cancellation in Stripe. Access continues according to the cancellation terms shown there. Canceling billing does not automatically delete account or lead records; contact support for a deletion request.

Does Peak provide leads or run my ads?

Peak helps you capture and organize inquiries from your connected sources. It does not supply prospect lists, launch ads, or change your advertising budget when you create a connection.

Can I get help with setup?

Email support@peakleadplatform.com or use our contact form to ask about your workflow and connection setup.
