YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Peak Lead Platform ("Peak," "we," or "us") handles information through our website, accounts, campaign tools, and lead forms. Peak helps businesses capture, score, organize, and follow up on inquiries.

Privacy questions or deletion requests?

Email peakplatform181@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, business name, industry, account role, and sign-in information. Passwords are protected using salted password hashes.
  • Lead and campaign information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, location details, answers to lead-form questions, inquiry notes, campaign identifiers, referral and advertising attribution, lead scores, pipeline stages, and follow-up history. The information collected depends on the form and how the business configures its campaigns.
  • Billing information: subscription status, trial dates, billing customer and subscription identifiers, and related billing records. Stripe collects payment-card details through its checkout and billing portal; Peak does not store full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Technical information: session cookies, browser session storage, and hosting or security logs that may include IP addresses, browser information, request details, and timestamps.
  • Connected-service information: when you authorize a supported integration, the Page, form, campaign, and lead information needed for that connection, along with access credentials used to receive or retrieve leads.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide the service, attribute and score inquiries, display campaigns and follow-ups, manage subscriptions, send account or service messages, troubleshoot problems, and respond to requests.

For forms operated by Peak to collect inquiries about Peak, we use the submitted details to respond to the request described on the form. Data submitted through a form labeled as a connection test is used to verify delivery and display within the platform. Please use fictional test details in those forms.

3. Leads managed by customer businesses

If you submit an inquiry to a business that uses Peak, that business chooses its form questions, how it uses the inquiry, who can access its account, and whether to contact you. Peak processes those lead records to provide the service to that business. Its own privacy notice also applies. Contact that business first about its follow-up, marketing, or handling of your inquiry; you may also contact us for assistance identifying the relevant account.

4. Service providers and other disclosures

We use service providers to operate Peak, including Vercel for hosting, Neon/Postgres for database storage, Stripe for payments, and Resend for service email. These providers receive information needed to perform their functions. Connected advertising platforms, such as Meta or Google, process information under their own policies when their services are used.

Lead records are available to authorized users of the business account managing them. We may also disclose information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, protect the service and its users, or address fraud or security incidents.

Peak does not sell or rent account or lead information. Customer businesses remain responsible for their own use and sharing of the leads they manage.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Peak uses a session cookie to maintain sign-in and browser session storage for account-interface information. Advertising attribution, such as campaign parameters and click identifiers, may be stored with a submitted lead when provided. You can clear cookies or browser storage through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or affect account features.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for operating accounts and lead-management services and for relevant billing, troubleshooting, security, and legal needs. Canceling a subscription stops renewal according to its billing terms; it does not automatically delete the account or its lead records. Contact us to request account or lead-data deletion. Some records may need to be retained for billing, security, or legal reasons.

We use access controls and other technical safeguards to protect information. Access credentials stored for supported lead connections are encrypted. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our service providers operate.

7. Your choices and requests

You can update available profile and business details in your account, manage your subscription through Billing, and revoke a connected platform's access through that platform's settings. Revoking an integration stops its future authorized access but does not automatically remove lead records already imported.

To ask about your information or request access, correction, or deletion, email peakplatform181@gmail.com. Describe the request and the business or account involved. Do not send passwords, card details, or access tokens. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting on a request. The rights available to you depend on applicable law and our role in handling the information.

8. Children and third-party services

Peak is a business service and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us. Third-party websites, billing portals, and advertising platforms have their own privacy practices, which you should review when using them.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Where additional notice or consent is required for a change in how information is used, we will provide it rather than relying solely on an edit to this page.
